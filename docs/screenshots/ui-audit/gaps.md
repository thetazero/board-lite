# Shot gaps: UI states the clients can show that `npm run shots` never stages

Compared: every render branch of `web/tv.ts` and `web/phone.ts` (plus the helpers they call in `web/common.ts`) against the shot list in `src/shots/scenes.ts`. Items are ordered within each group, most likely to look broken first. Line numbers are from the tab-44 worktree at 89bb3e5.

How sizes work (src/shots/shots.ts:355): a shot is taken at the other sizes (TV 720p; phone @320, @landscape, @tablet) only if it has `sizes: true`, or the run passes `--every-size`. A shot with `then`, `live` or `fill` is taken at the default size only, even with `--every-size`. Those shots are in their own section at the end.

---

## TV (web/tv.ts)

1. **Fight banner with a crowded side: 5–6 raiders, many dragons each, a long list of defenders.** `battle()` tv.ts:404-410 puts one `.fighter` per dragon in `.bside`, and one per defender unit in `.bside.foes` (not stacked like `foeStack`). The `fled` rows go under the fighters (tv.ts:376-379). Only 3p and 4p fights are shot (raid-result-3p, raid-fight-3p, raid-fled-4p). A 6p raid where everyone commits could put 12+ fighters and a full defender roster on screen, which risks overflow or columns that scroll past the screen edge. Beams are placed with getBoundingClientRect (tv.ts:453), so they break if the box scrolls.
2. **Raid spoils for many winners.** tv.ts:381 puts one `<span>` per winner in `.spoils`, each with `gainChips`. Shots only cover a 3p win. With 4–6 winners, each taking a mixed share, the row can wrap badly or run off the screen.
3. **Raid commit and fight-or-bail screens at 6 players, long names.** In `renderRaid` (commit tv.ts:286-296, bail tv.ts:297-302), each raider row holds every raid-ready dragon as a 60px badge with its HP bar and name (`raiderDragon` tv.ts:266-269). Only tv-raid-commit-4p and tv-raid-bail-3p are shot, and neither at 720p. A 6p row of long-named players with 4+ dragons each is the likeliest place for the arena to overflow.
4. **Top bar during discard, with several players discarding and long names.** `phaseLabel` tv.ts:129 builds "Maximilianus, Wilhelmina W and Bartholomew discard down to the hand limit". tv-discard-4p is default size only and most likely has one discarder. This long string sits in `.topbar` beside the round, the goal and the join URL (tv.ts:202-207), and could crush or wrap the bar at 720p.
5. **In-game top bar with a Tailscale address.** tv.ts:206 adds `· Tailscale <b>…</b>` to `.roomcode`. Only the lobby is shot with `tailscale: true` (lobby-full-6p). In game, the bar would hold two full URLs plus the phase label, and 720p is the risk.
6. **Trade offers that include cards.** The offers panel (tv.ts:172, 201) prints `o.give`/`o.get` from `sideText` (src/state.ts:197-203), for example "2 stone, Standing Stones card, Driftwood Yard card". No scene offers a card: trade-4p and names-6p offer one resource. Long text, and nothing caps the panel's width.
7. **Many open trade offers at once.** The panel over the board's tray is one `<li>` per offer (tv.ts:172). The most shot is 2 (trade-4p). In 5–6p, every out-of-turn player can offer the acting player, so 5+ rows plus the acting player's own offers could cover much of the board.
8. **A player card with every badge: titles held and beasts slain.** In `.phead` (tv.ts:154-155, 162-167), each title and each slain beast adds a `.title-badge` between the name and the hand/points counts. Shots show at most one slain beast (beasts-slain-3p, peak-free-3p). Three titles plus two beasts plus a 12-character name can overflow the row. `fitSide` (tv.ts:228-239) then shrinks every card's font to compensate.
9. **Game-over tie-breaks.** tv.ts:472 renders `tiebreakNote` (common.ts:393-396): "Wins the tie with the most gold" or "Wins the tie by being earliest in turn order". The standings also gain `tie` tags (common.ts:385). Neither over-4p nor over-6p is staged as a tie. This adds an extra line in the end box, plus tags in each row.
10. **Game over with long names, and secret points in the standings.** `standingsHtml` (common.ts:380-390) adds `+N` hidden-points chips. over-6p uses short NAMES, so no scene ends a game with LONG_NAMES or a hidden Secret Hoard.
11. **Flat-map fallback (no WebGL).** tv.ts:187-192 and 200 render `mapSvg` plus two legend rows when `Board3D.supported()` is false. Shots always run with swiftshader WebGL (shots.ts:109), so this layout has never been seen. The board, legends and side columns are all laid out differently.
12. **Great-beast fight lost, and the Leviathan's fight.** `huntBanner` tv.ts:397-399 has a "The X holds" branch. Only a won Thunderbird fight is shot (tv-beast-fight-3p, tv-beast-slain-3p). The Leviathan never fights, and its unit art and name length in the banner are unseen.
13. **Back-to-back raids in one round (War Horn), after the first fight.** After a fight banner, `updateOverlay` (tv.ts:463-481) shows the second raid's vote, with `raidPips` (tv.ts:260-263) on the Commit and Fight-or-bail headings too. tv-two-raids-4p shoots the first vote only. Pips beside a long heading, and the banner-to-vote handoff, are unseen.
14. **Dragon states on the board's player cards: Fought, Healed in N rounds, Hungry.** `dragonState` (common.ts:256-261) is used by `tvDragon` (tv.ts:146-149). After a fauna clear, dragons read "Fought" with an HP bar. No board shot is taken after a clear (beasts-3p shoots the banner). "Healed in 2 rounds" and the growth dots in roomy (≤4p) cards make the line longer.
15. **Player cards at the dragon cap in a small game.** In ≤4p, `roomy` adds growth dots (tv.ts:147). 2p late games could hold 6+ dragons per player, and late-2p has no dragon-count condition. `fitSide` then shrinks the cards to the minimum font, and things could still overflow.
16. **Feed with long event lines.** `eventHtml` (common.ts:176-215) for `raidEnd` with several winners and chips, the `title` change, `cleared`, `again` and `target … by coin flip`, sliced to 5 lines and trimmed by height (tv.ts:181-183, 219-222). Long lines wrap and push the older ones out. Nothing stages a feed full of multi-winner raid ends at 720p.
17. **TV error toasts other than the guest Start.** tv.ts:112-114 and 503-509: "Add a bot" or the × remove button from a guest TV ("Only the TV or the first player can add bots."), and a failed `boot` (tv.ts:511). Only tv-guest-start is shot, at default size. The toast is `max-width: 90vw` (style.css:96).
18. **Production banner with a long round of rolls at 720p, or in 2p.** `rollBanner` tv.ts:333-356 is shot for 4p and 6p (live, default size only). Players with many dice (late game, several production buildings) make a row of many `dieFace` at 2.4em, which could overflow a row.
19. **Lobby without a LAN address, or Tailscale only.** `joinUrl` falls back to `location.hostname` (tv.ts:69-71), and `tailscaleUrl` is null without LAN (tv.ts:74-77). Low risk: different URL lengths in `.join-box .url`.

## Phone (web/phone.ts)

1. **Keep-one-of-two at small sizes.** `keepView` phone.ts:603-615 shows two big cards with their rule text side by side, and the hand fan stays docked below (phone.ts:261). phone-keep-3p is default size only. At @320 and @landscape (390px tall) this is very likely cramped or overflowing.
2. **Card zoom of a dragon card at @landscape/@320.** `cardZoom` + `dragonStats` (phone.ts:365-396) make the tallest zoom: 3 stage rows, a growth line and a sentence. phone-card-open-dragon is default only, and only a building card zoom is shot at every size (phone-card-open-3p). On a 390px-tall landscape screen, the actions and the ‹ › nav are likely off screen.
3. **Toasts at small sizes, and long engine errors.** `toast` phone.ts:853-860 shows any rule error, named. Some are long, like "Answer Maximilianus's offer o3 and Wilhelmina W's offer o4 first: accept or decline." (src/engine.ts:985) or "It's X's turn. Out of turn you can only offer, accept, decline or cancel trades." (engine.ts:980). The only toast shot is phone-toast-4p, which uses `then`, so it's never taken at other sizes. At @320 the toast can cover the talk bar and the hand. Long names are never in a toast.
4. **Several offers to you at once.** In `actionView` phone.ts:316-318 each incoming offer is a full block with Accept/Decline, stacked above the tabs. The most shot is one (trade-incoming-4p, trade-acting-4p). In 6p the acting player can get 5 at once, which pushes the turn's buttons far down at @320.
5. **Offers that include cards.** The offer text comes from `sideText` (src/state.ts:197-203): "Standing Stones card, Driftwood Yard card", in the incoming and outgoing rows (phone.ts:317, 320). No scene offers a card. Long bold text sits inline with the Withdraw button.
6. **"Answer the offer to end your turn" at @320.** phone.ts:332: a long label on the big disabled button. phone-trade-acting-4p is default only.
7. **A big hand at @320: 11–12 cards, or one of every card.** `handFan` phone.ts:400-413 with `longname` cards, and `fitHand` (phone.ts:275-287). phone-production-hand-2p, phone-civic-hand-2p and phone-every-card are default only. Overlap and tilt at 320px wide are unverified.
8. **Choosing a dragon for Growth Elixir or Healing Potion at @320.** phone.ts:382 makes one button per dragon, labelled "Frost Wyrm 2 (young, 12/12)" (`dragonLabel` phone.ts:415-418), in a `.row`. Shot at default size only (phone-zoom-elixir, phone-zoom-potion). Many dragons with long labels in a row will wrap or overflow.
9. **Windfall: choosing a resource.** The `needs === 'resource'` branch (phone.ts:381) shows the row of resource icon buttons. Windfall is in fauna-3p's hand, but no shot opens it, and every-card doesn't open it either. This branch has never been seen.
10. **Fight or bail with many allies.** `raidView` bail phone.ts:584-597: `.allies` lists every other raider's dragons as 34px badges (phone.ts:587-588). Shot in 3p and 4p only, default size. In a 6p raid, 5 allies with several dragons each push Fight/Bail off screen at @320 and @landscape.
11. **Raid commit with many raid-ready dragons, at small sizes.** phone.ts:574-582 makes one tall button per dragon, then "Send N dragons" at the bottom. phone-raid-commit-4p is default only, and the other commit shot (phone-raid-stay-home-5p, with sizes) likely has 1–2 dragons. With 6+ dragons, the Send button is far below the fold.
12. **Game over for a non-winner at small sizes, and ties.** `gameOver` phone.ts:626-639 adds "You finish 2nd (tied) with 18" and a tiebreak note (phone.ts:634-635) over a 4–6 row standings. Only the winner is shot at every size, and no tie is staged. With LONG_NAMES or 6p, the screen gets long at @320 and @landscape.
13. **Status line during a raid with a long target name at @320.** `status` phone.ts:236-237 shows the raid icon, the target name and a public/secret tag in one line. The raid shots that are taken at every size are the vote ones (vote-5p) and phone-raid-stay-home-5p, so target names vary by seed.
14. **The resource bar with big numbers and long income.** `header` phone.ts:207-225: five tokens, each with an income line like "−1 +2d6 +1d4" (`incomeText` common.ts:244-249) and the `minus` class, plus the hidden-points chip. Late-6p at every size covers a typical case. Two-digit counts with three dice sizes at @320 are unverified.
15. **After keeping a card, waiting on the others.** In the cards phase with `me.choice` empty, the body is `''` (phone.ts:255). The screen is just the status "Waiting for Ada and Bram." over the hand. The keep-3p scene only shoots a player still choosing. This state could look empty or unfinished.
16. **Hunting at small sizes: the beast picker and the dragon choice.** The `#hunt` picker's `about` is a `.hint` paragraph instead of the card strip (phone.ts:766-770). `huntView` phone.ts:472-489 has a long heading like "Clear the Cave Bear on Wilhelmina W's Driftwood Yard" (phone.ts:479, 485). The fauna shots are default only. The heading with a long name and a long building name can wrap badly at @320.
17. **Talk bar with a long latest message at @320, and a long-name "to" list.** `renderTalk` phone.ts:690-717: `.latest` holds the last message, up to 300 characters (phone.ts:655), with a private "to … (private)" form (phone.ts:686). The `<select>` options read "Maximilianus only" (phone.ts:699). Only talk-4p is shot (short names), and only the open sheet at every size.
18. **Talk sheet with no messages yet.** phone.ts:706: the placeholder "No one has said anything yet. Players who aren't in the room (like Claude) only hear what you send here." Never shot open while empty.
19. **Unread badge at small sizes.** phone-talk-unread-4p uses `then`, so it's default only. Where the `.unread` count lands at @320 next to a long latest line (phone.ts:651, 712-713) is unseen.
20. **Leaving to the join form with "You are not in this game."** phone.ts:111 calls `leaveToJoin` (phone.ts:147-154) with this message in game. Only the lobby removal message (phone-removed) is shot.
21. **"No room for another dragon: build a roost first." in Build & bank.** phone.ts:435, when the roost is full. phone-build-bank-3p and phone-basics-panel-2p are early-game, so the egg row is probably just affordable or not. With this line, the panel gets longer at @320.
22. **Bank with a multiplier: times > 1, and the disabled Trade button.** phone.ts:439-440. phone-bank-picks taps + once, default size. Larger chips in the Trade button ("12 stone → 3 gold") at @320 are unverified.
23. **Trade compose with several cards offered.** phone.ts:456-458 names each offered card in bold in `.tpick`. phone-trade-compose-4p offers one card. Three or four long card names wrap the panel.
24. **Cards dealt into the hand mid-flight.** `dealt` and the animation delay (phone.ts:127-141, 355). Never caught mid-animation. Low risk, but it is the first thing a player sees each round.
25. **"Waiting for the table." fallback.** phone.ts:243 when `waitingOn` is empty. Transient, low risk.

## Never seen at the other sizes: shots with `then` / `live` / `fill` (default size only, even with --every-size)

TV, never at 720p:
- New building popping up: tv-build-3p (`live`, frames). tv.ts board update via board3d.
- Production roll banner, mid-roll and all landed, 4p and 6p: tv-production-4p, tv-production-done-6p (`live`). `rollBanner` tv.ts:333-356.
- A raid result straight after the next round's production: tv-banners-3p (`live`). tv.ts:315-326, 489-501.
- Raid fight mid-replay, won, lost, with a bailer flown home: tv-raid-fight-3p, tv-raid-result-3p, tv-raid-lost-3p, tv-raid-fled-4p (`live`). `fightBanner`/`battle` tv.ts:363-450.
- Everyone bails, and no raid this round: tv-raid-bailed-2p, tv-raid-nobody-3p (`live`). tv.ts:320.
- Great-beast fight, mid-fight and slain: tv-beast-fight-3p, tv-beast-slain-3p (`then`). `huntBanner` tv.ts:389-402.

So no TV banner of any kind (roll, fight, hunt, no-raid) has ever been seen at 1280×720, where the battle and production layouts have the least height.

Phone, never at @320, @landscape or @tablet:
- Error toast: phone-toast-4p (`then`). phone.ts:853-860.
- Talk bar with an unread count: phone-talk-unread-4p (`then`). phone.ts:710-713.
- Join form after being removed from the lobby: phone-removed (`then`). phone.ts:107, 147-154.
- Join form errors (bad code, name taken, blank name, game started, room full): phone-join-bad-code, phone-join-name-taken, phone-join-blank-name, phone-join-started, phone-join-full (`fill`). `renderJoin` phone.ts:65-93, `.error` phone.ts:75. Long server messages like "That game has already started and every seat is taken." (src/server/room.ts:101) are never seen at @320.
- A resumed game, just joined into an open seat: phone-resume-join (`fill`).

Default size only, but `--every-size` would cover them (no `sizes`, and no then/live/fill). These are the most worth adding `sizes: true` to, because the layout is dense: phone-keep-3p, phone-card-open-dragon, phone-card-open-action, phone-zoom-elixir, phone-zoom-potion, phone-trade-acting-4p, phone-trade-incoming-4p, phone-raid-commit-4p, phone-raid-bail-3p, phone-discard-many, phone-every-card, phone-production-hand-2p, phone-game-over-loser, phone-fauna-send-3p, phone-beast-send-3p, phone-fauna-pick-3p. On the TV: tv-raid-commit-4p, tv-raid-bail-3p, tv-discard-4p, tv-trade-4p, tv-game-over-4p, tv-beasts-3p, tv-fauna-3p.
